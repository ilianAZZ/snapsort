About

    

      
A local web app that sorts the memories of a Snapchat data export, one
        swipe at a time. It reads the archives without unpacking them, writes the
        date and place into what you keep, and can glue split videos back
        together.

      
Nothing leaves your machine. No account, no telemetry, no network
        call — and your source archives are never modified.
